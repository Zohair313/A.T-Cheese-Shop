## Global Generation Standard (Apply to Every Prompt)

- **Consistent Camera & Optics:** Shot on Canon EOS R6, Canon RF 50mm f/1.8 STM lens at f/2.8 for gentle natural depth of field.
- **Lighting Setup:** Single directional commercial window or 45-degree diffused overhead light source with natural shadow falloff. No synthetic dual-tint rim lighting.
- **Realism & Textures:** Tangible food physics, micro-creases in parchment interleafing paper, cold condensation droplets, authentic dairy grain, micro-scratches on brushed 304 food-grade stainless steel surfaces.
- **Composition:** Off-center, documentary angle, natural kitchen workbench perspective, realistic un-retouched food prep documentary style.
- **Color Grading:** Authentic muted palette: dairy cream, buttermilk ivory, deep aged cheddar ochre, matte parchment, neutral brushed metal. No oversaturated cartoon yellows or HDR glow.
- **Strict Universal Negative Prompt:** `illustration, cartoon, 3D render, CGI, plastic or over-smooth look, glossy ad look, HDR, oversaturated, symmetrical studio setup, floating objects, extra fingers, hand deformities, text, letters, logos, watermark, brand names, artificial reflections, neon glow`
- **Output Workflow:** Generate 4 variants per brief, inspect for edge distortion and artifacting, select the most documentary-accurate frame, downscale to target WebP format at 85% quality.
